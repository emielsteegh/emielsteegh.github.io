I was born in a rural part of Limburg. I'm passionate about data, NLP, and complex problems. I work as a Machine Learning Engineer at VodafoneZiggo. On the side I like building projects, repairing stuff, [running](https://www.strava.com/athletes/62134196), [reading](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/152849827-emiel), and making things — whether that's 3D printing, designing, or tinkering with code.

I have a Master's in Data Science from the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/) and a Bachelor's in Creative Technology from the [University of Twente](https://www.utwente.nl/), where I learned to bridge the gap between engineering, business and the real world. I enjoy working with AI to make things better.

I run my own small business and have worn many hats — freelancer, back-end developer, wedding photographer, and [teaching assistant](/teaching/). During my time at the University of Twente I spent a full-time year as secretary of the board of [Study Association Proto](https://www.proto.utwente.nl/page/oldfarts#board8).

If you want to have a chat, coffee, beer or whisky, you can reach me through any of the icons at the bottom of this page!
